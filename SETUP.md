# Developer Set-Up
Mini Metro runs on **Unity 2022.3.62f2** and we are using BepInEx:
https://docs.bepinex.dev/articles/user_guide/installation/index.html

Extract the downloaded `.zip` into your Mini Metro folder

Afterwards, you have to unstrip the game:
https://hackmd.io/@ghorsington/rJuLdZTzK

This step is required for it to build properly, follow the instructions here to unstrip the game.

After everything, Launch Mini Metro through Steam then close it to set up the Bepin Environment
